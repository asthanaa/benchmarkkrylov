\begin{abstract}
We present a compressed Krylov framework together with two practical algorithms
for strongly correlated quantum many-body problems. In each Krylov step, the
Hamiltonian acts on a compressed propagation vector, the resulting update is
orthogonalized against the current Krylov basis, compressed under fixed
propagation and storage budgets, and incorporated through a projected
generalized eigenvalue problem in the resulting compressed, generally
nonorthogonal Krylov space. A thick-restart variant preserves the accuracy of
this budget-limited Krylov procedure by retaining several low-lying Ritz
vectors between cycles, while a selected-configuration-interaction refinement
built on the compressed Krylov support further reduces the resources required
to reach highly accurate solutions. We demonstrate these algorithms for
stretched N$_2$ and Cr$_2$ active spaces of up to 22 orbitals, and show that
they recover the exact solution of the 49-qubit synthetic Hamiltonian used as
a benchmark for quantum Krylov calculations in a regime where other selected
configuration-interaction methods have limited reach.
\end{abstract}
