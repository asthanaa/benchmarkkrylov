Convergence of CCIK-guided exactized refinement for the public 49Q\_v1 instance. The blue circle marks the variational strict-restart CCIK seed obtained with Krylov dimension 17, $K_{\mathrm{prop}} = 1000$, $K_{\mathrm{store}} = 1000$, $n_{\mathrm{root}} = 5$, and 16 restart cycles. The orange squares denote the CKCI-$n$ refinement sequence, where $n$ labels the number of post-CCIK shell-growth refinements: CKCI-0 is the exact diagonalization of the original CCIK-selected determinant pool, CKCI-1 is obtained after one top-512 Hamiltonian-connected shell expansion, and CKCI-2 after two such expansions. This refinement pathway reaches an absolute energy error of $3.73 \times 10^{-9}$ at 130013 determinants. Teal diamonds show the experimental SKQD points digitized from the published figure; paper points reported at zero error are clipped to the plot floor for display on the logarithmic axis. The dashed horizontal line marks the $10^{-8}$ target.
