Convergence of strict-restart CCIK and CKCI-$n$ refinement for the public 49Q\_v1 synthetic Hamiltonian. Blue circles denote the Pareto frontier obtained from screened CCIK runs; the seed selected for refinement used Krylov dimension 17, $K_{\mathrm{prop}}=1000$, $K_{\mathrm{store}}=1000$, $n_{\mathrm{root}}=5$, and 16 restart cycles. Orange squares show the CKCI-$n$ sequence initialized from this seed: CKCI-0 is the exact diagonalization of the 8522-determinant CCIK-selected pool, while CKCI-1 and CKCI-2 are obtained by adding one and two Hamiltonian-connected shells generated from the largest 128 amplitudes, followed by exact diagonalization. CKCI-2 reaches an absolute energy error of $3.73 \times 10^{-9}$ with 37483 determinants, below the $10^{-8}$ target shown by the dashed line. Teal diamonds show the published experimental SKQD points; zero-error points are clipped only for display on the logarithmic axis.
